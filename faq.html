Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Convertly and our free browser-based image conversion tools.


1. Is Convertly completely free?

Yes. All available image conversion tools on Convertly are free to use without registration or subscriptions.


2. Are my images uploaded to your servers?

No. Convertly processes supported image conversions directly inside your browser. Your files stay on your device.


3. Which image formats are supported?

  • WEBP to JPG
  • JPG to PNG
  • PNG to JPG
  • HEIC to JPG

4. Is there a file size limit?

Most conversions depend on your browser and device memory rather than server limits. Very large files may take longer to process.


5. Does Convertly work on mobile devices?

Yes. Convertly works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers using modern web browsers.


6. Which browsers are supported?

  • Google Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Brave Browser

7. Do I need to create an account?

No. Convertly does not require registration or login to use its image conversion tools.


8. Is my privacy protected?

Yes. Privacy is one of our core principles. Since supported conversions happen locally in your browser, your images are not stored on our servers.


9. Why should I use Convertly instead of other converters?

  • Free to use
  • No software installation
  • No registration
  • Fast browser processing
  • Privacy-first approach
  • Simple interface

10. Will more tools be added?

Yes. We plan to add more image conversion and optimization tools over time, including image compression, resizing and additional file formats.
