# Convertly

Fast. Free. Private.

Convert JPG, PNG, WEBP and HEIC images directly in your browser.

---

## 🚀 Features

- JPG to PNG
- PNG to JPG
- WEBP to JPG
- HEIC to JPG

- 100% Browser-Based Processing
- No Upload Required
- No Signup Required
- Privacy First
- Fast & Secure
- Works on Desktop & Mobile

---

## 🔒 Privacy

All image processing happens directly in your browser.

Your files are **never uploaded** to any server.

---

- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
