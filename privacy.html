Last Updated: July 2026

1. Your Privacy Matters

Convertly is designed with privacy in mind. All supported image conversions are performed directly inside your web browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers during the conversion process.

Image files remain on your device while using Convertly. Processing takes place locally in your browser using modern web technologies. Once you close the browser tab, temporary processing data is automatically removed by your browser.

We do not collect, store, or access the images you convert.

6. Data Security

Because image processing happens locally on your device, your files are not transmitted to our servers. We continuously work to provide a safe and secure browsing experience.

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.


8. Changes to This Policy
